5. Pronunciation Dictionaries (downloaded on first use, not bundled)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phoneme text-to-speech voices use word pronunciations from ipa-dict
(https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict), released under the MIT
license; some languages carry their own source license (CC BY, CC BY-SA,
Unicode). The engine downloads a language's file from the pinned commit
listed in engine/onnx/src/audio/tts/data/lexicon_sources.json the first
time it is needed. Languages under GPL or non-commercial licenses are
never downloaded.
